AAHAR 2027 • NEW DELHI

Participation Policy & Terms

European Wineries Delegation • Organised by Media India Group (MIG) • Supported by ITPO, Govt. of India

How It Works

Participation Workflow

Follow these steps to confirm your winery's participation and showcase arrangements:

Registration & Package Selection

Submit winery registration details, select Option 1 (€600) or Option 2 (€2,000), and specify wine portfolio.

Participation Confirmation & Invoice

Media India Group confirms eligibility, reviews technical details, and issues invoice with Razorpay payment link.

Sample Preparation & Shipping

Prepare wine samples, product technical sheets, and brochures according to Indian import guidelines.

Sample Receipt & Customs Clearance (Deadline: 30 Jan 2027)

All wine samples must be physically received in India and customs-cleared by 30 January 2027.

AAHAR 2027 Exhibition & B2B Meetings (2–6 March 2027)

Product showcase at the collective European stall and pre-scheduled buyer meetings in New Delhi.

Key Logistics Deadline: 30 January 2027. All wine samples must be physically received in India and cleared through customs by 30 January 2027 to allow preparation and buyer outreach before AAHAR 2027 (2–6 March, New Delhi).
Packages & Inclusions

Participation Packages

Two clear options structured for European wineries entering the Indian market.

Option 1

Product Showcase

€600 + applicable taxes
  • Wine samples displayed at the AAHAR stall in India.
  • Product showcase and brochure distribution opportunity.
  • Shipping costs and applicable duties/taxes are borne by the participant.
Option 2 • Premium

Full Presence & B2B

€2,000 + applicable taxes
  • Dedicated table space and seating for winery representative(s).
  • Three guaranteed meetings with potential distributors/buyers.
  • Meetings arranged at AAHAR or elsewhere in Delhi.
  • Includes the €600 participation fee.
  • Travel, accommodation, visa and personal expenses not included.
Terms & Obligations

Participation Terms & Conditions

  • Participation is subject to formal written confirmation and invoice payment with Media India Group (MIG).
  • Sample Logistics: Participants are responsible for all international shipping costs, Indian customs duties, labelling compliance, and clearance charges for wine samples.
  • Receipt Deadline: All selected wine samples must be physically received in India and cleared by Indian customs by 30 January 2027.
  • Exporters remain responsible for providing product technical sheets and complying with all applicable export, import, labelling, and food safety standards.
  • Cancellation & Refund: Registration fees are non-refundable, except in the event that AAHAR 2027 is officially cancelled by ITPO.
  • Option 2 includes 3 guaranteed meetings with potential buyers/distributors. Meeting schedules and venues in Delhi are coordinated directly by MIG.
  • Travel, accommodation, visa, local transfers, and personal expenses are not included in either participation package.
