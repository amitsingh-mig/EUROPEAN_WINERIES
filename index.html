Market Background & Opportunity

The Indian Wine Market Opportunity & European Heritage

The Indian wine market is experiencing unprecedented growth, driven by rising disposable incomes, changing lifestyles, and an increasing preference for premium beverages among urban consumers. This steady rise in domestic consumption, coupled with the rapid expansion of the hospitality sector and the emergence of wine tourism, has positioned India as one of the most promising emerging markets globally.

Europe stands as the historic heartland and global leader of the wine industry, boasting centuries-old traditions, unmatched quality standards, and iconic wine-producing regions. While European labels enjoy high prestige worldwide, there is also a surging appreciation for quality wines across Indian metropolitan hotels, fine-dining restaurants, and upscale retail channels.

Beyond direct trade, this initiative builds a bridge for bilateral collaboration connecting European winery producers with vetted Indian importers, distributors, HORECA procurement leaders, and institutional buyers eager for new regional labels.

Landmark Policy Catalyst

EU–India Free Trade Agreement: Substantial Tariff Phase-Down

Historic Market Access

The timing of AAHAR 2027 is uniquely significant in light of the recently concluded India–European Union Free Trade Agreement. Under this agreement, import tariffs on European wines in India—previously as high as 150%—will be significantly reduced in phased tiers, dramatically enhancing affordability and consumer access across the subcontinent.

Strategic Value

Why Participate at AAHAR 2027?

AAHAR is South Asia’s largest and most established international food, beverage, and hospitality trade fair, organized by ITPO (Govt. of India). Joining the collective European pavilion offers unparalleled market entry advantages:

  • European Wine Visibility in India

    Prominent collective presence showcasing European wine heritage, distinct grape varietals, and regional terroirs directly to India's wine community.

  • Access to Potential Buyers and Distributors

    Engage directly with established Indian wine importers, regional distributors, and modern trade procurement managers seeking fresh European supply lines.

  • Business Networking Opportunities

    Pre-scheduled meetings, one-on-one trade dialogues, and strategic networking sessions arranged by Media India Group's bilingual coordination team.

  • Introduction to the Indian Hospitality Sector

    Tap directly into India’s 5-star hotel chains, luxury dining properties, private members' clubs, and sommeliers representing 32% of total domestic wine consumption.

  • Collective Support for Smaller European Producers

    Dramatically reduce the cost, risk, and logistical barriers of entering India through shared infrastructure, coordinated marketing, and on-ground representation.
