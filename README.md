# EUROPEAN_WINERIES

## European Wineries at AAHAR 2027
**New Delhi, India | 2–6 March 2027**  
Organized by **Media India Group (MIG)** with the support of **India Trade Promotion Organization (ITPO), Government of India**.

---

### Overview
A dedicated landing page and participation proposal platform for European wineries showcasing their wines and connecting with Indian importers, distributors, and hospitality leaders at AAHAR 2027 in New Delhi.

---

### Key Features
- **Modern Dark-Mode Hero Section**: 2-column layout with glassmorphism bento grid, ITPO endorsement badge, and animated floating grape & vine leaf artwork.
- **Floating Sticky Header**: Glass navigation bar with smooth-scroll section links and direct Google Form registration CTA.
- **Participation Pathways**:
  - **Option 1: Product Showcase** (€600) — Dedicated wine sample display, guided tastings, and qualified contact reports.
  - **Option 2: Full Presence & B2B** (€2,000) — Walk-in tasting counter, pre-scheduled B2B buyer meetings, and full on-site representation.
- **Official Registration Integration**: Embedded Google Form for direct winery registrations and buyer inquiries.
- **Modern Contact & Footer**: Responsive dark grid contact section featuring European liaison coordinates and brand footer.

---

### Tech Stack
- **HTML5 & Semantic Markup**
- **CSS3** (Custom Properties, Grid, Flexbox, Glassmorphism, Fluid Typography)
- **Bootstrap 5.3 & Bootstrap Icons**
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Sticky navbar scroll detection, smooth scrolling)
